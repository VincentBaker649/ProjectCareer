Why did I choose this career?

I chose to work towards a software developer because I had to research it. I had a project once in which I had to make a website about a career I wanted to persue, I had nothing in mind but had an idea of the area.
I knew I enjoyed conding and making games and apps so when I came across the page for software developer it caught my interest and I ended up choosing it. It involves developing software,(shocker) and working with a team not just for the coding but also design and testing.

The more realistic reasons?

It also just happens to be a job that is in demand and one of the biggest reasons is, they get paid a lot to do their job well. I talking hundreds of thousands of dollars a year in just the upper middle side.
The hours are pretty normal for the average job and I could work from home sometimes if needed.
Money is not all the reason though, this is a job I could see really enjoying and making a good career out of. Just so happens to have great pay.
